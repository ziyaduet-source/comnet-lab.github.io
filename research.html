Research Areas

Four core research thrusts unifying wireless communications, energy systems, industrial AI, and blockchain security.

Research Area 01

Wireless Network Optimization for 5G and Beyond

We focus on boosting edge throughput through multi-connectivity and Coordinated Multi-Point (CoMP) techniques, allowing simultaneous connections to multiple nodes for resilient, high-throughput service. Integrating 5G terrestrial and satellite communications further expands coverage, delivering seamless high-speed connectivity to remote areas.

This combined approach enhances network reliability, supports diverse applications like IoT, and ensures resilient, ubiquitous access in the evolving 5G landscape. Our algorithms span resource allocation, handover management, mobility robustness optimization, and load balancing in heterogeneous network (HetNet) environments.

  • Resource Allocation & MAC Protocol Design
  • Handover Optimization & Mobility Management
  • Load Balancing in HetNets (LTE, 5G)
  • Coordinated Multi-Point (CoMP) Transmission
  • 5G Satellite-Terrestrial Integration
  • Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS)
  • mmWave Channel Modeling & Beamforming

Research Area 02

Energy ICT

The Internet of Things (IoT) enhances renewable energy networks by enabling efficient energy resource management and real-time monitoring across solar, wind, and other systems. A secure network for energy networks is vital, protecting against cyber threats and ensuring reliable power flow.

Machine Learning (ML) applications enable predictive insights, such as forecasting energy demand and optimizing grid balance. Together, IoT, secure networking, and ML create adaptive, efficient, and resilient renewable energy networks. Our work includes LSTM and Transformer-based forecasting models and cooperative multi-agent RL for battery management.

  • Solar & Wind Power Forecasting (LSTM, Transformers)
  • Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Optimization
  • IoT-enabled Smart Grid Monitoring
  • State-of-Charge (SoC) Estimation via Kalman Filter
  • Cooperative Multi-Agent DQN for Battery Packs
  • Renewable Energy Network Security
  • Packetized Energy Frameworks

Research Area 03

Smart Factory

Smart factories leverage advanced AI techniques for real-time fault diagnosis and process optimization, enhancing productivity and reducing downtime. By using AI algorithms, these factories can automatically detect, predict, and address equipment faults, ensuring continuous operation.

AI-driven systems also optimize production flows, adapt to demand fluctuations, and improve resource utilization. This integration of AI not only boosts efficiency but also supports a resilient, adaptive manufacturing environment essential for Industry 4.0. Our CNN-based real-time classification systems have been applied to diesel engine fault detection and marine engine load classification.

  • CNN-based Real-Time Fault Detection & Classification
  • Diesel Engine Load Classification (1D vs 2D CNN)
  • Industrial AI & Predictive Maintenance
  • Process Optimization & Flow Adaptation
  • Time-Series Sensor Data Analysis

Research Area 04

Blockchain in Wireless Communication

Blockchain is enhancing security in wireless networks by using decentralized, tamper-resistant ledgers to prevent data breaches. Integrating IoT with blockchain ensures secure, verifiable communication between connected devices.

Additionally, decentralized management of wireless data networks minimizes reliance on central authorities, boosting network resilience. Machine learning further optimizes blockchain protocols, enhancing efficiency and scalability for dynamic wireless environments. We study sharding blockchain channel modeling for next-generation IoT and RAN resource allocation secured by blockchain.

  • Sharding Blockchain-enabled Wireless IoT Channel Modeling
  • Blockchain-enabled RAN Resource Allocation
  • Decentralized Wireless Data Network Management
  • IoT Security via Distributed Ledgers
  • ML-Optimized Blockchain Protocols

Research Area 05

Vehicular & Sensor Networks / Bio-Signal Processing

ComNeT also conducts research on vehicular communications (V2V, V2I, In-Vehicle), sensor-based indoor positioning systems, and bio-signal processing for health monitoring. These projects span MAC protocol design for emergency message forwarding in vehicular environments and wavelet-based PPG signal processing for wearable health devices.

Our indoor positioning work combines ultrasonic and RF signals for accurate 3D localization in indoor environments, with applications in healthcare and industrial monitoring.

  • V2V Emergency Message Forwarding Protocols
  • Indoor Positioning (Ultrasonic & RF fusion)
  • PPG Motion Noise Cancellation (Wavelet Transform)
  • IoT for Healthcare Access (Deaf Patient Systems)
  • Wearable Bio-Sensor Systems
