Research

Our Core Research Areas

We work across four major research thrusts, bridging theory and real-world application for next-generation networks.

📡

Wireless Network Optimization for 5G and Beyond

Resource allocation, handover management, load balancing, and CoMP techniques for 5G/6G heterogeneous networks integrated with satellite systems.

Learn more →
⚡

Energy ICT

IoT-enabled renewable energy management, solar and wind power forecasting with ML, battery energy storage system (BESS) optimization, and smart grid security.

Learn more →
🏭

Smart Factory

AI-driven real-time fault diagnosis, CNN-based engine load classification, and process optimization for Industry 4.0 manufacturing environments.

Learn more →
🔗

Blockchain in Wireless Communication

Decentralized, tamper-resistant security for wireless networks and IoT using sharding blockchain, with ML-optimized protocols for dynamic environments.

Learn more →
