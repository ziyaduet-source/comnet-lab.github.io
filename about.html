About ComNeT Lab

Communications and Networking Laboratory — bridging academia and industry since 2010.

Our Mission

Bridging Academia and Industry in Next-Generation Networks

The Communications and Networking Laboratory (ComNeT) was established in 2010 within the School of Electrical Engineering at the University of Ulsan, Korea. Supervised by Prof. Sungoh Kwon, ComNeT mentors undergraduate and graduate students to scale up their practical and professional knowledge while building strong connections between academia and industry.

In ComNeT, students are working on mobile communication, sensor networks, and bio-signal processing. We actively co-work with industry partners including Hyundai, ETRI, and Sinkpuls for new inventions, and collaborate internationally with IMDEA Networks (Spain), ÉTS (Canada), and KNRF.

The lab has produced numerous PhD graduates who have gone on to positions at leading universities, research institutes, and companies worldwide. We provide a rigorous yet supportive research environment where students develop both theoretical depth and practical engineering skills.

Research Interests

Prof. Kwon's Research Focus

Wireless/wireline communication networks and their applications across the full networking stack.

📡
5G/6G MAC & Routing Protocols

Design and evaluation of routing and MAC protocols for 3G/4G/5G/6G networks and IoT.

🔧
Network Optimization

Optimization problems in communication networks — resource allocation, scheduling, load balancing.

📊
Network Capacity Analysis

Network capacity of multi-hop wireless networks and vehicular-to-vehicle (V2V) systems.

🌱
Energy Efficiency

Energy efficiency in communication networks, renewable energy forecasting, battery management.

🚗
Vehicular Networks

V2V, V2I and in-vehicle communication protocols; mobility and location management.

📍
Mobility Management

Mobility and location management in multi-hop wireless networks; handover optimization.
